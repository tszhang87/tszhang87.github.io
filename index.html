01 / About

From research
to running systems.

I’m interested in how distributed systems behave under real constraints: changing traffic, finite capacity, and the need to keep serving through failures.

My work sits at the intersection of content delivery, network access security, and reliability engineering. I’m interested in protecting access to network services through abuse prevention, traffic controls, and rate limiting. I earned my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Boston University in 2017.

This site brings together my research, engineering interests, and technical writing.
